How we organise your journey↗
Before you go

A few things to know.

How can I join a journey?+

Yes. Tell us which journey interests you and when you could travel. We will discuss whether a shared journey is possible for your dates. Availability is confirmed individually.

Where do I find dates and prices?+

The starting prices on this website give you an initial guide. Send us your preferred dates, route and budget. We confirm availability, your total price and included services in your personal offer before you book.

Which language will be spoken?+

We work with English-speaking and German-speaking local guides; French-speaking accompaniment can also be arranged. Tell us your preference. We confirm the language for your chosen journey and dates before you book.

How much walking and driving should I expect?+

The routes combine road travel, walks and, on some journeys, boats or domestic flights. Share your experience and preferred pace so we can clarify walking conditions, transfers and accommodation with you.

Who can help before and during the journey?+

Hasina and the Meet You team are your contact for planning. Before departure, we clarify your local contacts and how to reach support during the journey.

Are international flights included?+

International flights are not included in our tour prices. Domestic flights are included only where listed in your personal offer. Our flight partner can help with international tickets at preferential conditions, subject to dates and availability. Confirm arrival and departure with us before buying tickets.
