About us & planning your journey

Meet You in Madagascar is a local travel company based in Antananarivo. Hasina and the team help you plan your route and coordinate practical details on the island. A forest walk, a conversation along the way, a swim at the end of the day: we want to leave room for the moments you will remember together.

From your first idea to departure↓
01

People you can talk to

Hasina and the team are your starting point for questions about the journey. Tell us what excites you and what you are unsure about. We discuss the route, accommodation and travel arrangements together, so you can understand what your days might look like before making a decision.

02

Adventure at your pace

Long walks or shorter trails? More time in the rainforest or a few days by the sea? Our journeys give you a starting point, and we discuss possible adaptations around your interests, time and budget. When you want to travel with others, we check what is possible for your dates.

03

Time to experience the island

Madagascar asks you to slow down and stay curious. We consider driving stages as well as the places you want to see, leaving room for breaks and shared experiences. Respect for the people you meet and the landscapes you explore belongs in the everyday rhythm of the journey.

From your first idea to departure

  1. 01

    Start with your ideas

    Send us your possible dates, number of travellers, interests and approximate budget. A favourite route helps, but you do not need a finished plan. Tell us your preferred travel language and how active you would like the days to be.

  2. 02

    Shape the route together

    We discuss a suitable itinerary and the practical choices: travel times, walks, rest days, accommodation and transport. Ask about anything that matters to you, from a particular activity to the comfort you want after a day outdoors.

  3. 03

    Read your personal offer

    Your written offer sets out the proposed programme, accommodation, transport, included services and price. We confirm availability and guiding language for your dates before you book. International flights are arranged separately; our flight partner can propose preferential conditions depending on dates and availability.

  4. 04

    Confirm the details, then look forward

    Take the time to review the offer and clarify your questions. Once the arrangements are confirmed, we coordinate arrival and the next practical steps with you. You know who your contact is and which arrangements have been agreed for your journey.

Details confirmed on request

A little flexibility goes a long way

On the map, two places can look close. On the road, the journey may take longer. Weather and road conditions can affect a stage, so a realistic pace matters as much as the list of highlights. We discuss the demands of your route before you decide.

  • ✓Tell us about your walking experience, comfort preferences and any needs that could affect planning.
  • ✓Ask which services, meals and activities are included, and which costs you should plan separately.
  • ✓Agree the language of your journey in your offer; English- and German-speaking local guides can be requested.
Good to know

Before you set off

When should I travel?+

The right season depends on your route and activities. Tell us your possible dates; we discuss weather and access when shaping the itinerary.

Can I come on my own?+

Yes, enquire with your dates and preferred route. We check whether travelling with others is possible. A shared departure and its availability are confirmed individually.

Which language will my guide speak?+

Request English- or German-speaking local guides when you enquire. We confirm the language before booking; the website language alone does not determine it.

Are flights included?+

International flights are organised separately. Domestic flights may form part of a route; your offer specifies what is included. Coordinate arrival and departure with us before purchasing tickets.

How demanding is the journey, and what will it cost?+

Walking, driving and accommodation vary by route. Share your experience, comfort preferences and budget to discuss suitable options. Your personal offer confirms the price and services.

How do I prepare, and who can I contact?+

Clarify practical preparations with us and consult current official travel information before departure. We agree your local contacts and how to reach assistance during the journey.

What would your Madagascar look like?

Start with the places that draw you in, the time you have and the people you want to share it with. We will help you turn those first ideas into a plan you can picture.
