The journey

Travel from the green waterways of the Pangalanes Canal to the limestone formations of the Tsingy. Forest, village life and rugged landscapes give this route its rhythm.

21 days · East & west

Your journey, stage by stage

Open a stage to discover the places, experiences and practical details.

Days 1–2Journey to Antananarivo+

Depart Europe and arrive in the capital for your first hotel night.

Days 3–4Andasibe and the calls of indris+

Travel through the eastern hills via Madagascar Exotic to Andasibe. The programme includes an evening outing, a forest walk of around three hours and a visit to the lemur islands.

Days 5–8By boat along the Pangalanes+

From Mahanoro, travel for three days by small boat along the Pangalanes Canal. Village and market visits reveal life along the water. This stage includes two nights camping between the canal and ocean, with meals prepared by the accompanying cook.

Days 9–10Ranomafana rainforest+

Leave the canal for the southern highlands and Ranomafana. Daytime and evening walks offer opportunities to look for bamboo lemurs, sifakas and nocturnal forest wildlife.

Days 11–14Via Antsirabe to the west coast+

Cross the highlands to Antsirabe, then continue via Miandrivazo and the Avenue of the Baobabs to Morondava. A free beach day offers a pause; a visit to a fishing village among the mangroves is optional.

Days 15–16The limestone pinnacles of Bemaraha+

Reach the Tsingy of Bemaraha by off-road vehicle and ferry on a track stage of around 200 kilometres. The following day, explore the limestone landscape on a full-day walk with bridges and climbing sections.

Days 17–19Manambolo, Kirindy and baobabs+

Paddle through the Manambolo gorges in a pirogue, then explore Kirindy’s dry forest, where you may be lucky enough to see a fossa. Return to Morondava via sunset at the Avenue of the Baobabs, followed by a free beach day.

Days 20–21Return flights via Antananarivo+

Fly domestically to Antananarivo. After a final night in the capital, continue your journey back to Europe.

A suggested route, planned around you. We confirm accommodation and the final programme in your personal offer.
