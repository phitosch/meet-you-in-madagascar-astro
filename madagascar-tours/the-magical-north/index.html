The journey

Explore Montagne d’Ambre, the Red Tsingy and Ankarana before crossing to Nosy Be and Nosy Iranja. Rainforest trails and island time, with the volcanic landscapes of Ampefy along the way.

13 days · North

Your journey, stage by stage

Open a stage to discover the places, experiences and practical details.

Day 1Arrival in Antananarivo+

Arrive in the capital for a first night before flying north.

Days 2–3Diego Suárez and the Mer d’Émeraude+

Fly to Diego Suárez, with time to explore the town or the beaches of Ramena and Orangea. The next day brings a boat trip to the Mer d’Émeraude.

Day 4Montagne d’Ambre rainforest+

Visit the national park’s rainforest, waterfalls and lakes. Along the trails, look for chameleons and other wildlife of this humid mountain forest.

Days 5–6Red Tsingy and Ankarana+

Travel by off-road vehicle to the Red Tsingy and onwards to Ankarana. The following day, explore the reserve’s limestone pinnacles and cave system.

Days 7–10Nosy Be and Nosy Iranja+

Cross to Nosy Be. The suggested island combination includes an outing with an overnight stay on Nosy Iranja and free time on Nosy Be for swimming, walking and relaxation.

Days 11–12Ampefy’s volcanic landscape+

Fly to Antananarivo and continue west to Ampefy, stopping at Lemurs Park. A walk of around three hours takes in waterfalls and the landscape around the geyser.

Day 13Journey back to Europe+

Travel back to Europe, with transfers and flight connections coordinated around your dates.

A suggested route, planned around you. We confirm accommodation and the final programme in your personal offer.
